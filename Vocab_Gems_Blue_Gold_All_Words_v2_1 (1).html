A quick field guide

How to play Vocab Gems

Choose a word jewel on the left, then its matching meaning card on the right. You may also select the meaning first. Correct pairs disappear, and the remaining pieces fall down. Match words, not just colors.

Color gives you a little strategy.

The jewels have four blue-and-gold varieties, each with its own symbol. Make three correct matches of the same jewel color consecutively to earn a 300-point color-trio bonus. A different color starts a new trio. A mistake or hint resets the trio.

Keep the streak alive.

A correct match earns 100 points, plus 5 extra points for each previous match in your streak, up to a 100-point streak bonus. A wrong match subtracts 50 points and resets the streak. A hint subtracts 100 points and resets the streak. Scores cannot fall below zero. Finishing a board earns 200 points. No points are added just for ending a round.

Copy your score as an image.

After finishing a round, click Copy score image, then paste into a place that accepts images. The blue-and-gold PNG includes the round score, best score on this browser for the same list and settings, unit/game name, accuracy, streak, coverage, settings, and finish date. Review rounds are marked as practice and do not update high scores. Download image saves the same PNG when clipboard access is unavailable. Clipboard permission and image pasting depend on the browser and destination. This game writes the image only when you click Copy; it never reads your clipboard. The image is a practice record, not a verified assessment.

Timed or untimed.

Challenges run for 2, 3, or 5 minutes. Words cycle through the whole list before repeating. Untimed mode ends once you have matched every pair once. Lists with repeated terms or definitions use separate boards for those entries to avoid identical visible choices. The pairs in the word list define the accepted answers.

Make it yours.

Open Teacher studio, select + New word list, name it, and paste your pairs. Copying two columns from Excel or Google Sheets works. You can also use hola | hello, hola = hello, or a two-column CSV. A header row such as Term,Definition is optional. Minimum: 3 different pairs. Maximum: 1,000. Text-based lists only in this version.

Give each assignment its own game.

Download all-words game creates one HTML file with the selected list built in. In Teacher studio, use Download unit pack (ZIP) for the full-list game plus smaller practice games. Split evenly or pick the exact words for each game. Students get a fixed list, not a vocabulary menu. Share that file through a district-approved method. Students need to open the actual HTML file in a browser; a document preview may not run it. Their device policies must permit local HTML and JavaScript. This is a file, not a hosted website or live link.

Keep a backup.

Browser storage is a convenience, not your only backup. Download teacher copy embeds all saved lists in a fresh HTML file. Back up all lists creates a JSON file you can restore with Import file. Data and best scores are local to this browser and do not sync across devices. No student names are requested or transmitted.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls; Enter or Space selects. Arrow keys move within a board. Pause hides both boards, and switching tabs automatically pauses the game. Crystal sounds start with your first Play click. Use Sound to mute, the volume slider to adjust loudness, or the Sparkles checkbox to reduce visual effects. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Long tile text can scroll; hovering or focusing a tile shows its full-text tooltip.
